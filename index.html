कैसे काम करता है • How it works

  1. Request / Call — अनुरोध करें: Fill the booking form below or call the coordinator with your need, area and time slot.
  2. Coordinator matches — समन्वयक चुनता है: The coordinator picks a nearby worker with the right skills from the register and confirms with you on phone.
  3. Worker arrives — काम होता है: The worker is guided to your address by phone (they only need a basic mobile phone) and completes the work.
  4. Pay worker directly — सीधे भुगतान: You pay the worker directly in cash or UPI. The platform takes only a small flat ₹49 booking fee — no hidden charges, no commission cut from the worker's pay.

⚖️ Wage floor — read this

Bihar's statutory minimum wage for unskilled workers is ₹436 per day (effective 1 April 2026; ₹428/day from Oct 2025). A full day's worker pay on this platform must never go below ₹436. Guide rates on this page are minimums for fair pay, not targets to undercut.

🦺 Basic safety guidance

  • Households: confirm the worker's name with the coordinator before letting anyone in; keep valuables secured; never ask a worker to do unsafe electrical, height or chemical work without proper gear.
  • Workers: never pay any registration or "job confirmation" fee to anyone — registration here is free; share your live location with family when going to a new address; you may leave any site that feels unsafe and call the coordinator.
  • Both: agree the pay and the task list on the coordinator's call before work starts. Report problems to the coordinator the same day.

📋 Compliance notes (headlines, not legal advice)

  • Bihar minimum-wage rules apply, including for domestic work; hourly wage = daily ÷ 8; equal pay for men and women.
  • Contract Labour Act licensing questions arise at 20+ contract workers — the pilot stays far below this.
  • Bihar's Platform-Based Gig Workers (Social Security & Welfare) Act, 2025 may add a welfare levy — to be verified with legal counsel before any commercial launch.
  • Workers can be supported to register on e-Shram (unorganised worker database).
